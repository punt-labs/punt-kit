\section{Introduction}

An engine holds one record per Claude Code session.  A session is named by its
\texttt{session\_id}; each run of it (a first start, or a \texttt{--resume})
has its own main-thread process id, and the process id changes on resume while
the \texttt{session\_id} does not.  The record may be live, dormant (its lease
lapsed) or ended (SessionEnd seen; durable state kept for a retention period).
Both Open and a call carrying a handle the session's record does not own are
get-or-create keyed by \texttt{session\_id}.

Live-client resources (callbacks, subscriptions, pending deliveries) belong to
a \emph{run}, not to the session: each is registered by the process that is
the record's current main pid.  A lapse keeps them (the same run may come
back); a SessionEnd or retention expiry releases them; an Open or call from a
\emph{different} pid is a new run and releases the previous run's resources
before the record goes live.  A revived ended record holds none.

The model is flat and bounded: two sessions, three process ids, three handles.
A record is keyed by its handle.

\begin{description}
 \item[I1] At most one record per session.
 \item[I2] A SessionEnd from a run that has been replaced never ends the
   record: an ended record was ended by the session's latest run.
 \item[I3] A session whose durable state exists, and whose record has not
   expired, has a record and that record holds the state.
 \item[I4] Dormancy never releases live-client resources; an ended record
   holds none.
 \item[I5] Every record's current main pid is the pid of the most recent Open
   (or call) for its session.
 \item[I6] Every resource a record holds belongs to its current run.
\end{description}

Handles are injective across records by construction (a record is keyed by its
handle); a collision shows as an overwritten record, which I3 reports.

\section{Basic Types}

\begin{zed}
  SID ::= sa | sb \\
  PID ::= pa | pb | pc \\
  HND ::= ha | hb | hc \\
  STATE ::= live | dormant | ended
\end{zed}

\section{State}

\texttt{hs} is the set of records, each named by its handle.  \texttt{resown}
gives, for a record that holds live-client resources, the pid of the run that
registered them.  The last five components are ghost history, used only to
state the invariants: \texttt{kept} is the dormant records that held resources
when their lease lapsed; \texttt{ever} relates a session to every pid that has
run for it; \texttt{lastrun} gives its latest run; \texttt{dursid} is the set
of sessions with durable state that has not legitimately expired.

\begin{schema}{Engine}
  hs : \power HND \\
  hsid : HND \pfun SID \\
  hst : HND \pfun STATE \\
  hpid : HND \pfun PID \\
  dur : \power HND \\
  resown : HND \pfun PID \\
  endby : HND \pfun PID \\
  kept : \power HND \\
  ever : SID \rel PID \\
  lastrun : SID \pfun PID \\
  dursid : \power SID
\where
  \dom hsid = hs \\
  \dom hst = hs \\
  \dom hpid = hs \\
  dur \subseteq hs \\
  \dom resown \subseteq hs \\
  kept \subseteq hs \\
  \dom endby = \dom (hst \rres \{ ended \}) \\
  \dom lastrun = \dom ever
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Init}
  Engine~'
\where
  hs' = \emptyset \\
  hsid' = \emptyset \\
  hst' = \emptyset \\
  hpid' = \emptyset \\
  dur' = \emptyset \\
  resown' = \emptyset \\
  endby' = \emptyset \\
  kept' = \emptyset \\
  ever' = \emptyset \\
  lastrun' = \emptyset \\
  dursid' = \emptyset
\end{schema}

\section{Safety}

\begin{schema}{Safety}
  Engine
\where
  \forall a, b : hs | hsid~a = hsid~b @ a = b \\
  \forall h : hs | hst~h = ended @ endby~h = lastrun (hsid~h) \\
  (\forall s : dursid @ s \in \ran hsid) \land
    (\forall h : hs | hsid~h \in dursid @ h \in dur) \\
  (\forall h : kept @ hst~h = dormant \land h \in \dom resown) \land
    (\forall h : hs | hst~h = ended @ h \notin \dom resown) \\
  \forall h : hs @ hpid~h = lastrun (hsid~h) \\
  \forall h : \dom resown @ resown~h = hpid~h
\end{schema}

\section{Operations}

A pid may speak for a session if the session has no history, or it is the
session's latest run, or it is a fresh run (a resume).  Every Open or call
makes its pid the latest run.

\begin{schema}{PidOk}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID
\where
  s? \notin \dom ever \lor p? = lastrun~s? \lor p? \notin ever \limg \{ s? \} \rimg \\
  ever' = ever \cup \{ s? \mapsto p? \} \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \oplus \{ s? \mapsto p? \}
\end{schema}

ReviveBase reuses the session's record in any retained state, makes it live
and clears any end mark.  It says nothing of resources: that is the business
of ReviveSame and ReviveNew.

\begin{schema}{ReviveBase}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  hs' = hs \\
  hsid' = hsid \\
  hst' = hst \oplus \{ h! \mapsto live \} \\
  endby' = \{ h! \} \ndres endby \\
  kept' = kept \setminus \{ h! \}
\end{schema}

The same run comes back: its resources, if any, stay.  A new run: the
previous run's resources are released first.

\begin{schema}{ReviveSame}
  \Delta Engine; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  hpid~h! = p? \\
  resown' = resown
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{ReviveNew}
  \Delta Engine; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  hpid~h! \neq p? \\
  resown' = \{ h! \} \ndres resown
\end{schema}

\begin{zed}
  Revive \defs ReviveBase \land (ReviveSame \lor ReviveNew)
\end{zed}

\begin{schema}{Repin}
  \Delta Engine; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  hpid' = hpid \oplus \{ h! \mapsto p? \}
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Store}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  dur' = dur \cup \{ h! \} \\
  dursid' = dursid \cup \{ s? \}
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{NoStore}
  \Delta Engine
\where
  dur' = dur \\
  dursid' = dursid
\end{schema}

A created record starts live and holds no resources.

\begin{schema}{CreateCore}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  s? \notin \ran hsid \\
  h! \notin hs \\
  hs' = hs \cup \{ h! \} \\
  hsid' = hsid \cup \{ h! \mapsto s? \} \\
  hst' = hst \cup \{ h! \mapsto live \} \\
  hpid' = hpid \cup \{ h! \mapsto p? \} \\
  resown' = resown \\
  endby' = endby \\
  kept' = kept
\end{schema}

\begin{zed}
  OpenReuse \defs PidOk \land Revive \land Repin \land NoStore \\
  OpenCreate \defs PidOk \land CreateCore \land NoStore \\
  OpenSess \defs OpenReuse \lor OpenCreate
\end{zed}

\begin{schema}{KnownHandle}
  h? : HND; h! : HND
\where
  h? = h!
\end{schema}

The presented handle is not owned by this session's record: it names no record
(free) or another session's record (foreign).

\begin{schema}{NotMine}
  Engine; s? : SID; h? : HND
\where
  (h? \mapsto s?) \notin hsid
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{FreeHandle}
  Engine; h? : HND
\where
  h? \notin hs
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{ForeignHandle}
  Engine; h? : HND
\where
  h? \in hs
\end{schema}

A call with a handle its session owns renews.  Any other handle resolves by
\texttt{session\_id}: to the session's record if it has one; otherwise a record
is created, under the presented handle if that is free, under a fresh handle if
the presented one belongs to another record.  Either way the call stores
durable state.

\begin{zed}
  CallKnown \defs PidOk \land Revive \land Repin \land Store \land KnownHandle \\
  CallReuse \defs PidOk \land Revive \land Repin \land Store \land NotMine \\
  CallCreateFree \defs
    PidOk \land CreateCore \land Store \land KnownHandle \land FreeHandle \\
  CallCreateFresh \defs PidOk \land CreateCore \land Store \land ForeignHandle \\
  CallTool \defs CallKnown \lor CallReuse \lor CallCreateFree \lor CallCreateFresh
\end{zed}

A run registers live-client resources for its record.

\begin{schema}{Register}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  hst~h! = live \\
  resown' = resown \oplus \{ h! \mapsto hpid~h! \} \\
  hs' = hs \\
  hsid' = hsid \\
  hst' = hst \\
  hpid' = hpid \\
  dur' = dur \\
  endby' = endby \\
  kept' = kept \\
  ever' = ever \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \\
  dursid' = dursid
\end{schema}

A lapse is dormancy: the record and its resources stay.

\begin{schema}{LeaseLapse}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  hst~h! = live \\
  hst' = hst \oplus \{ h! \mapsto dormant \} \\
  kept' = kept \cup (\{ h! \} \cap \dom resown) \\
  hs' = hs \\
  hsid' = hsid \\
  hpid' = hpid \\
  dur' = dur \\
  resown' = resown \\
  endby' = endby \\
  ever' = ever \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \\
  dursid' = dursid
\end{schema}

SessionEnd may arrive from any pid that ever ran for the session.  It ends
the record only if the pid is the record's current main pid.

\begin{schema}{EndMatch}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  p? \in ever \limg \{ s? \} \rimg \\
  hst' = hst \oplus \{ h! \mapsto ended \} \\
  resown' = \{ h! \} \ndres resown \\
  kept' = kept \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  endby' = endby \oplus \{ h! \mapsto p? \} \\
  hs' = hs \\
  hsid' = hsid \\
  hpid' = hpid \\
  dur' = dur \\
  ever' = ever \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \\
  dursid' = dursid
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{EndStale}
  \Xi Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  p? \in ever \limg \{ s? \} \rimg \\
  hpid~h! \neq p?
\end{schema}

\begin{zed}
  SessionEnd \defs EndMatch
\end{zed}

\begin{schema}{RetentionExpire}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  hst~h! \in \{ ended, dormant \} \\
  hs' = hs \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  hsid' = \{ h! \} \ndres hsid \\
  hst' = \{ h! \} \ndres hst \\
  hpid' = \{ h! \} \ndres hpid \\
  dur' = dur \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  resown' = \{ h! \} \ndres resown \\
  kept' = kept \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  endby' = \{ h! \} \ndres endby \\
  ever' = ever \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \\
  dursid' = dursid \setminus \{ s? \}
\end{schema}

Resume is not a separate operation: a new run with a new pid calls
\texttt{OpenSess} (or \texttt{CallTool}), and \texttt{PidOk} admits the fresh
pid.
