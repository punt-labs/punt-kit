\section{Introduction}

An engine holds one record per Claude Code session.  A session is named by its
\texttt{session\_id}; each run of it (a first start, or a \texttt{--resume})
has its own main-thread process id, and the process id changes on resume while
the \texttt{session\_id} does not.  The record may be live, dormant (its lease
lapsed; live-client resources kept) or ended (SessionEnd seen; resources
released; durable state kept for a retention period).  Both Open and a call
carrying an unknown handle are get-or-create keyed by \texttt{session\_id}.

The model is flat and bounded: two sessions, three process ids, three handles.
A record is keyed by its handle, so \emph{one record per session} (I1) is the
non-trivial claim that no two handles map to one session.

\begin{description}
 \item[I1] At most one record per session.
 \item[I2] A SessionEnd from a run that has been replaced never ends the
   record: an ended record was ended by the session's latest run.
 \item[I3] A session whose durable state exists, and whose record has not
   expired, has a record and that record holds the state.
 \item[I4] Dormancy never releases live-client resources (and, structurally,
   live records hold them and ended records do not).
 \item[I5] Every record's current main pid is the pid of the most recent Open
   (or call) for its session.
\end{description}

\section{Basic Types}

\begin{zed}
  SID ::= sa | sb \\
  PID ::= pa | pb | pc \\
  HND ::= ha | hb | hc \\
  STATE ::= live | dormant | ended
\end{zed}

\section{State}

\texttt{hs} is the set of records, each named by its handle.  The last three
components are ghost history, used only to state the invariants:
\texttt{ever} relates a session to every pid that has run for it,
\texttt{lastrun} gives its latest run, and \texttt{dursid} is the set of
sessions with durable state that has not legitimately expired.

\begin{schema}{Engine}
  hs : \power HND \\
  hsid : HND \pfun SID \\
  hst : HND \pfun STATE \\
  hpid : HND \pfun PID \\
  dur : \power HND \\
  res : \power HND \\
  endby : HND \pfun PID \\
  ever : SID \rel PID \\
  lastrun : SID \pfun PID \\
  dursid : \power SID
\where
  \dom hsid = hs \\
  \dom hst = hs \\
  \dom hpid = hs \\
  dur \subseteq hs \\
  res \subseteq hs \\
  \dom endby = \dom (hst \rres \{ ended \}) \\
  \dom lastrun = \dom ever
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Init}
  Engine~'
\where
  hs' = \emptyset \\
  hsid' = \emptyset \\
  hst' = \emptyset \\
  hpid' = \emptyset \\
  dur' = \emptyset \\
  res' = \emptyset \\
  endby' = \emptyset \\
  ever' = \emptyset \\
  lastrun' = \emptyset \\
  dursid' = \emptyset
\end{schema}

\section{Safety}

\begin{schema}{Safety}
  Engine
\where
  \forall a, b : hs | hsid~a = hsid~b @ a = b \\
  \forall h : hs | hst~h = ended @ endby~h = lastrun (hsid~h) \\
  (\forall s : dursid @ s \in \ran hsid) \land
    (\forall h : hs | hsid~h \in dursid @ h \in dur) \\
  (\forall h : hs | hst~h = dormant @ h \in res) \land
    (\forall h : hs | hst~h = live @ h \in res) \land
    (\forall h : hs | hst~h = ended @ h \notin res) \\
  \forall h : hs @ hpid~h = lastrun (hsid~h)
\end{schema}

\section{Operations}

A pid may speak for a session if the session has no history, or it is the
session's latest run, or it is a fresh run (a resume).  Every Open or call
makes its pid the latest run.

\begin{schema}{PidOk}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID
\where
  s? \notin \dom ever \lor p? = lastrun~s? \lor p? \notin ever \limg \{ s? \} \rimg \\
  ever' = ever \cup \{ s? \mapsto p? \} \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \oplus \{ s? \mapsto p? \}
\end{schema}

Revive reuses the session's record in any retained state, makes it live,
restores its resources, and clears any end mark.

\begin{schema}{Revive}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  hs' = hs \\
  hsid' = hsid \\
  hst' = hst \oplus \{ h! \mapsto live \} \\
  res' = res \cup \{ h! \} \\
  endby' = \{ h! \} \ndres endby
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Repin}
  \Delta Engine; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  hpid' = hpid \oplus \{ h! \mapsto p? \}
\end{schema}

Variant of defect (b), the literal reading of section 4.2: only Open updates
the record's current main pid; a call carrying the new run's main\_agent\_id
does not.

\begin{schema}{KeepPid}
  \Delta Engine
\where
  hpid' = hpid
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{Store}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  dur' = dur \cup \{ h! \} \\
  dursid' = dursid \cup \{ s? \}
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{NoStore}
  \Delta Engine
\where
  dur' = dur \\
  dursid' = dursid
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{CreateCore}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  s? \notin \ran hsid \\
  h! \notin hs \\
  hs' = hs \cup \{ h! \} \\
  hsid' = hsid \cup \{ h! \mapsto s? \} \\
  hst' = hst \cup \{ h! \mapsto live \} \\
  hpid' = hpid \cup \{ h! \mapsto p? \} \\
  res' = res \cup \{ h! \} \\
  endby' = endby
\end{schema}

\begin{zed}
  OpenReuse \defs PidOk \land Revive \land Repin \land NoStore \\
  OpenCreate \defs PidOk \land CreateCore \land NoStore \\
  OpenSess \defs OpenReuse \lor OpenCreate
\end{zed}

\begin{schema}{KnownHandle}
  h? : HND; h! : HND
\where
  h? = h!
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{UnknownHandle}
  Engine; h? : HND
\where
  h? \notin hs
\end{schema}

A call with a held handle renews; a call with a handle no record holds goes
through get-or-create under the session.  Either way the call stores durable
state.

\begin{zed}
  CallKnown \defs PidOk \land Revive \land KeepPid \land Store \land KnownHandle \\
  CallUnknownReuse \defs
    PidOk \land Revive \land KeepPid \land Store \land UnknownHandle \\
  CallUnknownCreate \defs
    PidOk \land CreateCore \land Store \land KnownHandle \land UnknownHandle \\
  CallTool \defs CallKnown \lor CallUnknownReuse \lor CallUnknownCreate
\end{zed}

\begin{schema}{LeaseLapse}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  hst~h! = live \\
  hst' = hst \oplus \{ h! \mapsto dormant \} \\
  hs' = hs \\
  hsid' = hsid \\
  hpid' = hpid \\
  dur' = dur \\
  res' = res \\
  endby' = endby \\
  ever' = ever \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \\
  dursid' = dursid
\end{schema}

SessionEnd may arrive from any pid that ever ran for the session.  It ends
the record only if the pid is the record's current main pid.

\begin{schema}{EndMatch}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  p? \in ever \limg \{ s? \} \rimg \\
  hpid~h! = p? \\
  hst' = hst \oplus \{ h! \mapsto ended \} \\
  res' = res \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  endby' = endby \oplus \{ h! \mapsto p? \} \\
  hs' = hs \\
  hsid' = hsid \\
  hpid' = hpid \\
  dur' = dur \\
  ever' = ever \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \\
  dursid' = dursid
\end{schema}

\begin{schema}{EndStale}
  \Xi Engine; s? : SID; p? : PID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  p? \in ever \limg \{ s? \} \rimg \\
  hpid~h! \neq p?
\end{schema}

\begin{zed}
  SessionEnd \defs EndMatch \lor EndStale
\end{zed}

\begin{schema}{RetentionExpire}
  \Delta Engine; s? : SID; h! : HND
\where
  h! \in hs \\
  hsid~h! = s? \\
  hst~h! \in \{ ended, dormant \} \\
  hs' = hs \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  hsid' = \{ h! \} \ndres hsid \\
  hst' = \{ h! \} \ndres hst \\
  hpid' = \{ h! \} \ndres hpid \\
  dur' = dur \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  res' = res \setminus \{ h! \} \\
  endby' = \{ h! \} \ndres endby \\
  ever' = ever \\
  lastrun' = lastrun \\
  dursid' = dursid \setminus \{ s? \}
\end{schema}

Resume is not a separate operation: a new run with a new pid calls
\texttt{OpenSess} (or \texttt{CallTool}), and \texttt{PidOk} admits the fresh
pid.
